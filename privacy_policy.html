Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flappy Bird Mobile collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

  • Local Gameplay Data: High scores, coin balances, unlocked character skins, and mission progress are stored exclusively on your local device (using device local storage). This data never leaves your device.
  • Advertising & Device Identifiers: We use third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic data to serve relevant, family-friendly ads in accordance with Google's policies.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app incorporates third-party SDKs from Google AdMob. You can review Google's privacy practices here:

Google Advertising Privacy & Terms

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is family-friendly and designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All advertising served is configured to comply with Google Play Families Policy requirements.

4. Security

We prioritize the protection of any data stored on your device. Since game data is saved locally on your hardware, you can reset or delete all game progress anytime by clearing app data in your device settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at: support@flappybirdmobile.com
